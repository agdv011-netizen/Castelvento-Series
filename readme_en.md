## 🎬 Overview
Castelcutto Stories is an atmospheric visual novel that combines open world exploration, sandbox gameplay, RPG elements, quests, and life simulation in a living 1940s Italian coastal town. The game features hand-drawn watercolor and pencil art style inspired by mid-20th century European postcards.
### Core Concept
Heavily inspired by "Malèna" (2000), set in a fictional 1940s Italian coastal town with a lighter, sandbox-style narrative. The world exists independently of the player with dynamic schedules and daily routines.
### Main Features
- ✨ Fully hand-drawn artistic style
- 🗺️ Open world exploration with dozens of interactive locations
- 🎭 Non-linear story with multiple protagonists  
- 🌅 Dynamic day/night cycle and weather changes
- 🧩 Quests, puzzles and secrets to uncover
- 💼 Economy system, jobs and skill development
- ❤️ Character relationship system
- 📋 Interactive notebook and inventory management
- 📱 Full cross-platform support (PC and mobile)
---
## 🎮 Gameplay
### Immersive Onboarding
The game starts with an interactive introduction in Renato's bedroom. No UI is visible initially - players must click on the backpack (unlocks inventory), notebook (unlocks quest log), wardrobe (get dressed), and wall clock (unlocks time HUD) to reveal the interface.
### UI Design
- **Top HUD**: Vintage-style "notch" with time indicator (center), energy/stamina (left), money (right)
- **Floating buttons**: Notebook and Backpack icons that change appearance based on context
- **Direct interaction**: Game happens directly on location backgrounds, not in separate UI panels
### Core Loop
- Point & click sandbox exploration across various locations
- First-person background views with detailed interactions
- Actions cost time and energy resources
- Build relationships and influence character fates
- Complete quests and solve mysteries around town
---
## 🌟 Living World
Castelcutto continues existing without the player:
- Residents go to work on schedules
- Children ride bicycles through streets
- Theater prepares for evening shows
- Trains arrive at the station
- Church bells mark time
- Sun sets over the sea with lights turning on at night
Each of dozens of characters has their own personality, habits, daily schedule, social circle, and personal history.

## 🎨 Art & Audio
### Visual Style
Highly detailed watercolor and pencil sketches in warm sepia tones capturing 1940s Italian atmosphere. Each scene is manually stylized after vintage illustrations.
### Sound Design
Original soundtrack featuring Mediterranean sounds - ocean waves, bicycle bells, church bells, market noise, evening cafes, and old cinema ambiance.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js (App Router), React
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS for responsive design
- **State Management**: Zustand
- **Animations**: Framer Motion

1. Foundation (UI, basic navigation)

4. Character systems (NPCs, relationships)
5. Story integration (quests, dialogue trees)
